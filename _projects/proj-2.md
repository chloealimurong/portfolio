As a part of the New Hampshire Health & Air Quality team, I partnered with the New Hampshire Department of Environmental Services (NHDES) AirResources Division to explore how remote sensing can be used to monitor and map methane concentrations from landfills over time. We utilized data from various Earth observations to visualize trends in methane concentrations across five landfills in New Hampshire. The final products aim is to inform the NHDES about the various methods available to analyze and understand the impacts of methane emissions andconcentrations on surrounding communities.
